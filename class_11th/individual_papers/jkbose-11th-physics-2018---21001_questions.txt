SECTION A : OBJECTIVE & VERY SHORT ANSWER QUESTIONS
[Q. 01 to Q. 08 [8 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.01 [1 Mark] (Units & Dimensions):
     Which of the following pairs of physical quantities have the same dimensional formula [M L² T⁻²]?

     (A) Force and Torque
     (B) Work and Torque
     (C) Momentum and Impulse
     (D) Pressure and Surface Tension

Q.02 [1 Mark] (Kinematics - Projectile Motion):
     A projectile is fired at an angle of 30° with the horizontal with initial velocity 25 m/s. The acceleration at the highest point of its trajectory is:

     (A) 0 m/s²
     (B) g directed vertically downwards
     (C) g sin(30°) along the trajectory
     (D) g cos(30°) along the horizontal

Q.03 [1 Mark] (Laws of Motion - Friction):
     The limiting friction between two surfaces in contact depends on:

     (A) Apparent area of contact
     (B) Nature of the surfaces and the normal reaction
     (C) Relative speed of sliding surfaces
     (D) Shape of the surfaces

Q.04 [1 Mark] (Work Energy Power):
     A body of mass 2 kg is attached to a spring of spring constant k = 112 N/m. The potential energy stored when compressed by 0.1 m is:

     (A) 0.56 J
     (B) 11.20 J
     (C) 5.60 J
     (D) 1.12 J

Q.05 [1 Mark] (Rotational Dynamics):
     State the law of conservation of angular momentum and express the relation between torque (τ) and angular momentum (L).

Q.06 [1 Mark] (Gravitation):
     What happens to the acceleration due to gravity (g) as we move from the equator towards the poles of the Earth?

Q.07 [1 Mark] (Fluid Mechanics):
     State Bernoulli’s Theorem for the streamline flow of an ideal incompressible fluid.

Q.08 [1 Mark] (Thermodynamics):
     Define an adiabatic process and write the equation of state for an ideal gas undergoing an adiabatic change.

================================================================================
SECTION B : SHORT ANSWER TYPE QUESTIONS - I
[Q. 09 to Q. 12 [8 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.09 [2 Marks] (Kinematics - Vectors):
     Two vectors A and B have equal magnitudes of 10 units each. If the angle between them is 60°, find the magnitude and direction of their resultant vector.

Q.10 [2 Marks] (Circular Motion):
     Derive an expression for the optimum banking angle (θ) of a curved road without friction, in terms of speed (v), radius (r), and gravity (g).

Q.11 [2 Marks] (Gravitation - Escape Velocity):
     Define escape velocity. Write its mathematical formula and explain why the moon has no atmosphere.

Q.12 [2 Marks] (Oscillations - SHM):
     Show that the motion of a simple pendulum executing small oscillations is simple harmonic (SHM), and write its time period formula.

================================================================================
SECTION C : SHORT ANSWER TYPE QUESTIONS - II
[Q. 13 to Q. 17 [15 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.13 [3 Marks] (Work Energy Theorem):
     State and prove the Work-Energy Theorem for a variable force acting on a particle moving in one dimension.

Q.14 [3 Marks] (Rotational Motion - Moment of Inertia):
     State the Theorem of Parallel Axes and Theorem of Perpendicular Axes for moment of inertia with suitable mathematical equations and diagrams.

Q.15 [3 Marks] (Properties of Matter - Viscosity):
     State Stokes' Law for the viscous drag on a spherical body falling through a fluid. Hence derive the expression for its terminal velocity.

Q.16 [3 Marks] (Thermodynamics - Heat Engines):
     Describe the four stages of a Carnot cycle with the help of a neat Indicator (P-V) Diagram. Write the expression for the efficiency of a Carnot engine.

Q.17 [3 Marks] (Waves - Doppler Effect):
     Explain the Doppler Effect in sound waves. Derive an expression for the apparent frequency when a sound source moves towards a stationary observer.

Q.18 [5 Marks] (Projectile Motion & Vectors):
     A projectile is launched from the ground with an initial velocity 'u' making an angle 'θ' with the horizontal.
     (a) Derive the equation of its parabolic trajectory.
     (b) Derive the expressions for (i) Time of flight, (ii) Maximum height attained, and (iii) Horizontal range.
     (c) At what angle is the horizontal range maximum?

     State Newton's Second Law of Motion and establish the vector relation F = dp/dt = ma.
     Show that Newton's Second Law is the real law of motion from which both the First and Third Laws can be deduced.
     A rocket of initial mass 5000 kg ejects exhaust gas at 1000 m/s at a rate of 50 kg/s. Calculate the initial upward thrust.

Q.19 [5 Marks] (Surface Tension & Fluid Mechanics):
     Explain what is meant by Surface Tension and Surface Energy.
     Derive an expression for the excess pressure inside:
     (a) A liquid drop.
     (b) A soap bubble in air.
     Explain why small raindrops are spherical while large drops tend to flatten.
