Q.01 [1 Mark] (Basic Concepts of Chemistry):
     What is the total number of moles present in 44 grams of Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)? (Atomic masses: C = 12, O = 16)

     (A) 0.5 mole
     (B) 1.0 mole
     (C) 2.0 moles
     (D) 44 moles

Q.02 [1 Mark] (Structure of Atom):
     Which quantum number specifies the spatial orientation of an orbital in a given subshell?

     (A) Principal quantum number (n)
     (B) Azimuthal quantum number (l)
     (C) Magnetic quantum number (mₗ)
     (D) Spin quantum number (mₛ)

Q.03 [1 Mark] (Chemical Bonding):
     What is the hybridization and geometrical shape of the Methane (CH₄) molecule?

     (A) sp² - Trigonal Planar
     (B) sp³ - Regular Tetrahedral
     (C) sp³d - Trigonal Bipyramidal
     (D) sp - Linear

Q.04 [1 Mark] (Thermodynamics):
     For an isolated system, the change in internal energy (ΔU) is always equal to:

     (A) q
     (B) w
     (C) 0
     (D) ΔH

Q.05 [1 Mark] (Equilibrium):
     State Le Chatelier’s Principle regarding the effect of temperature and pressure on chemical equilibrium.

Q.06 [1 Mark] (Redox Reactions):
     Calculate the oxidation state of Manganese (Mn) in the Potassium Permanganate compound KMnO₄.

Q.07 [1 Mark] (Organic Chemistry - IUPAC):
     Write the IUPAC name of the compound: CH₃-CH(CH₃)-CH₂-CHO.

Q.08 [1 Mark] (Hydrocarbons):
     State Markovnikov’s rule for the addition of unsymmetrical reagents to unsymmetrical alkenes.

Q.09 [2 Marks] (Atomic Structure - de Broglie & Heisenberg):
     State Heisenberg’s Uncertainty Principle. Calculate the uncertainty in position of an electron moving with a velocity of 300 m/s with 0.001% accuracy.

Q.10 [2 Marks] (Periodic Properties):
     Explain why the first ionization enthalpy of Nitrogen (N) is higher than that of Oxygen (O), despite Oxygen having a higher nuclear charge.

Q.11 [2 Marks] (Chemical Equilibrium - Buffer Solutions):
     What is a buffer solution? Give an example each of an acidic buffer and a basic buffer.

Q.12 [2 Marks] (Organic Reaction Mechanisms):
     Explain the difference between Inductive Effect (+I / -I) and Electromeric Effect (+E / -E) with suitable examples.

Q.14 [3 Marks] (Molecular Orbital Theory):
