Q.01 [1 Mark] (Ecology & Environment):
     The structural and functional unit of the biosphere comprising biotic and abiotic components is called:

     (A) Biome
     (B) Ecosystem
     (C) Population
     (D) Community

Q.02 [1 Mark] (Natural Resources):
     Which of the following is an inexhaustible renewable energy resource?

     (A) Coal
     (B) Petroleum
     (C) Solar Energy
     (D) Natural Gas

Q.03 [1 Mark] (Air Pollution):
     Which secondary pollutant is a major constituent of photochemical smog?

     (A) Carbon Monoxide (CO)
     (B) Peroxyacetyl Nitrate (PAN)
     (C) Sulfur Dioxide (SO₂)
     (D) Lead (Pb)

Q.04 [1 Mark] (Ecosystem Food Chains):
     Define 10% Law of energy transfer proposed by Raymond Lindeman.
