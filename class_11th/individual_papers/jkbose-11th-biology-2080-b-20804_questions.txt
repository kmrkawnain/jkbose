SECTION A : BOTANY : PLANT DIVERSITY, ANATOMY, CELL BIOLOGY, AND PLANT PHYSIOLOGY
[Q. 01 to Q. 07 [35 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.01 [1 Mark] (Biological Classification):
     Five kingdom classification was proposed by:

     (A) Carolus Linnaeus
     (B) R.H. Whittaker
     (C) Ernst Mayr
     (D) Aristotle

Q.02 [1 Mark] (Plant Morphology):
     A sterile stamen is known as:

     (A) Staminode
     (B) Pistillode
     (C) Carpellode
     (D) Antheridium

Q.03 [2 Marks] (Cell Biology):
     Differentiate between Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria based on cell wall composition.

Q.04 [2 Marks] (Cell Division):
     What is crossing over? Name the sub-stage of Prophase-I of Meiosis in which crossing over occurs.

Q.05 [3 Marks] (Photosynthesis in Higher Plants):
     Explain the Hatch and Slack pathway (C₄ pathway) of carbon fixation with an anatomical and biochemical diagram.

Q.06 [3 Marks] (Plant Growth Regulators):
     Enumerate the physiological roles of Auxins and Gibberellins in agricultural and horticultural practices.

Q.07 [5 Marks] (Plant Anatomy / Physiology):
     Describe the Calvin cycle (C₃ pathway) in detail with a schematic diagram. Explain how many molecules of ATP and NADPH are required to produce one molecule of glucose.

     ----------------------------------------------------------------------
     OR
     Give an illustrated account of the internal structure of a Dicot Stem and compare it with a Monocot Stem.

SECTION B : ZOOLOGY : ANIMAL DIVERSITY, BIOMOLECULES, AND HUMAN PHYSIOLOGY
[Q. 08 to Q. 14 [35 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.08 [1 Mark] (Animal Kingdom):
     Which of the following phyla possesses water vascular system (ambulacral system)?

     (A) Arthropoda
     (B) Annelida
     (C) Echinodermata
     (D) Mollusca

Q.09 [1 Mark] (Biomolecules):
     Which of the following is a non-reducing sugar?

     (A) Glucose
     (B) Maltose
     (C) Sucrose
     (D) Lactose

Q.10 [2 Marks] (Circulation):
     Explain the physiological events occurring during the cardiac cycle in humans.

Q.11 [2 Marks] (Excretion):
     Explain the counter-current mechanism operated by Henle's loop and vasa recta in concentrating urine.

Q.12 [3 Marks] (Neural Control & Coordination):
     Describe the generation and conduction of a nerve impulse along an unmyelinated axon with resting and action potentials.

Q.13 [3 Marks] (Chemical Coordination):
     Explain the mechanism of hormone action of peptide hormones versus steroid hormones.

Q.14 [5 Marks] (Human Physiology):
     Describe the human respiratory system with a neat diagram and explain the transport of Oxygen (O₂) and Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) in blood.

     ----------------------------------------------------------------------
     OR
     Give a detailed account of the structure of a human nephron with a labelled diagram. Explain the mechanism of glomerular filtration, selective reabsorption, and tubular secretion.
