SECTION I : PHYSICS : OPTICS, ELECTRICITY, MAGNETIC EFFECTS, AND ENERGY
[Q. 1 to Q. 13 [Total: 32 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.01 [1 Mark] (Light - Optics):
     Define the principal focus of a concave mirror. What is the relation between its focal length (f) and radius of curvature (R)?

Q.02 [1 Mark] (Optics - Lens Power):
     A doctor prescribes a corrective lens of power -2.0 D. Find the focal length of the lens. Is the prescribed lens converging or diverging?

Q.03 [1 Mark] (Electricity):
     State Ohm's Law and express it in mathematical form relating Potential Difference (V), Current (I), and Resistance (R).

Q.04 [1 Mark] (Magnetism):
     Why don't two magnetic field lines ever intersect each other?

Q.05 [1 Mark] (Energy Sources):
     What is the role of a plane mirror and glass sheet in a box-type solar cooker?

Q.06 [2 Marks] (Optics - Refraction):
     A ray of light traveling in air enters obliquely into water. Does the light ray bend towards the normal or away from the normal? Explain why with reference to optical density.

Q.07 [2 Marks] (Human Eye Defects):
     A student unable to see clearly the words written on the blackboard placed at a distance of 3 m. Name the defect of vision the boy is suffering from. How can this defect be corrected?

Q.08 [2 Marks] (Electricity Numericals):
     Two resistors of resistance R₁ = 9 Ω and R₂ = 10 Ω are connected in: (a) Series, (b) Parallel. Calculate the equivalent resistance in each case.

Q.09 [3 Marks] (Optics - Ray Diagrams):
     An object 5 cm in length is placed at a distance of 20 cm in front of a convex mirror of radius of curvature 30 cm. Find the position, nature, and size of the image formed. Draw a neat ray diagram.

Q.10 [3 Marks] (Joule Heating & Power):
     State Joule's law of heating. An electric iron of resistance 20 Ω takes a current of 5 A. Calculate the heat generated in 30 seconds.

Q.11 [3 Marks] (Electromagnetic Induction):
     State Fleming's Left-Hand Rule and Fleming's Right-Hand Rule. Specify which rule is used to determine the direction of force on a current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field.

Q.12 [5 Marks] (Electric Motor & Generator):
     Explain with the help of a neat, labelled diagram the principle, construction, and working of an Electric Motor. What is the function of split rings (commutator) in an electric motor?

     ----------------------------------------------------------------------
     [OR CHOICE]
     Explain the principle and working of an AC Generator with a neat labelled diagram. What is the difference between Alternating Current (AC) and Direct Current (DC)? Why is AC considered advantageous for long-distance power transmission?

Q.13 [5 Marks] (Human Eye & Atmospheric Optics):
     (a) Draw a neat diagram of the Human Eye and label: Cornea, Iris, Pupil, Crystalline Lens, and Retina.
     (b) Explain with diagrams the cause and correction of Hypermetropia (far-sightedness).
     (c) Why do stars twinkle while planets do not twinkle?

     ----------------------------------------------------------------------
     [OR CHOICE]
     (a) What is dispersion of white light? Explain with a ray diagram how a glass prism disperses white light into its spectrum.
     (b) Explain the formation of a rainbow in the sky with a labelled diagram.
     (c) Why does the sky appear dark to an astronaut instead of blue?

SECTION II : CHEMISTRY : REACTIONS, ACIDS-BASES, METALS, CARBON, AND PERIODIC CLASSIFICATION
[Q. 14 to Q. 26 [Total: 32 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.14 [1 Mark] (Chemical Reactions):
     Why should a magnesium ribbon be cleaned with sandpaper before burning in air?

Q.15 [1 Mark] (Acids, Bases & Salts):
     Write the chemical name, chemical formula, and one important use of Bleaching Powder.

Q.16 [1 Mark] (Metals & Non-metals):
     Name one metal and one non-metal which exist in the liquid state at room temperature.

Q.17 [1 Mark] (Carbon Compounds):
     Write the IUPAC name and structural formula of the second member of the alkyne series (propyne).

Q.18 [1 Mark] (Periodic Classification):
     State Modern Periodic Law. How does the metallic character of elements vary down a group in the Modern Periodic Table?

Q.19 [2 Marks] (Chemical Equations):
     Balance the following chemical equations:
           (i) Fe + H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + H₂
           (ii) HNO₃ + Ca(OH)₂ → Ca(NO₃)₂ + H₂O

Q.20 [2 Marks] (Acids and Bases):
     Why does dry HCl gas not change the color of dry litmus paper, whereas aqueous hydrochloric acid does?

Q.21 [2 Marks] (Metallurgy):
     Differentiate between Roasting and Calcination with suitable balanced chemical equations.

Q.22 [3 Marks] (Ionic Bonding):
     (a) Write the electron-dot structures for Sodium (Na), Oxygen (O), and Magnesium (Mg).
     (b) Show the formation of Na₂O and MgO by the transfer of electrons.
     (c) Why do ionic compounds have high melting and boiling points?

Q.23 [3 Marks] (Carbon Homologous Series):
     (a) What is a homologous series? State any two characteristics of a homologous series.
     (b) Explain why carbon forms a vast number of compounds (tetravalency and catenation).

Q.24 [3 Marks] (Periodic Trends):
     An element 'X' has atomic number 17.
           (i) Write its electronic configuration.
           (ii) To which group and period does it belong in the Modern Periodic Table?
           (iii) State whether it is a metal or a non-metal and write the formula of its chloride.

Q.25 [5 Marks] (Carbon Chemistry - Ethanol & Ethanoic Acid):
     (a) How can ethanol and ethanoic acid be differentiated on the basis of their physical and chemical properties?
     (b) Explain the mechanism of the cleansing action of soaps with a neat labelled diagram of micelle formation.
     (c) What happens when ethanoic acid reacts with sodium hydrogen carbonate? Write the balanced chemical equation.

     ----------------------------------------------------------------------
     [OR CHOICE]
     (a) Explain the following chemical reactions with balanced equations:
         (i) Esterification reaction
         (ii) Saponification reaction
         (iii) Addition reaction of unsaturated hydrocarbons
     (b) Why are detergents preferred over soaps for washing clothes in hard water?

Q.26 [5 Marks] (Metals Extraction & Corrosion):
     (a) Explain the electrolytic refining of copper with a neat, labelled diagram.
     (b) What is an alloy? Give the composition and uses of Brass and Stainless Steel.
     (c) What is corrosion? Mention two effective methods to prevent rusting of iron.

     ----------------------------------------------------------------------
     [OR CHOICE]
     (a) What happens when Plaster of Paris is mixed with water? Write the chemical equation.
     (b) How is Sodium Hydroxide manufactured industrially by the Chlor-alkali process? Name the gases liberated at the anode and cathode.
     (c) Define universal indicator and pH. What is the pH of human blood and acid rain?

SECTION III : BIOLOGY (LIFE SCIENCES) : LIFE PROCESSES, CONTROL-COORDINATION, REPRODUCTION, HEREDITY, AND ECOLOGY
[Q. 27 to Q. 36 [Total: 20 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.27 [1 Mark] (Life Processes):
     What is the role of hydrochloric acid (HCl) and pepsin in the stomach during digestion?

Q.28 [1 Mark] (Control & Coordination):
     Name the plant hormone responsible for: (i) Promotion of cell division, (ii) Wilting of leaves / growth inhibition.

Q.29 [1 Mark] (Reproduction):
     What is vegetative propagation? Give one example of a plant that reproduces through leaves.

Q.30 [1 Mark] (Environment):
     What is the 10% law of energy transfer in an ecosystem? Who proposed this law?

Q.31 [2 Marks] (Respiration Comparison):
     Differentiate between Aerobic Respiration and Anaerobic Respiration with reference to oxygen requirement, site of reaction, and amount of energy released.

Q.32 [2 Marks] (Plant Transport):
     How are water and minerals transported in plants? Contrast the functioning of Xylem and Phloem tissues.

Q.33 [2 Marks] (Heredity & Genetics):
     How is the sex of a child determined in human beings? Explain with a genetic cross showing XX and XY chromosomes.

Q.34 [3 Marks] (Nervous System - Reflex Arc):
     What is a reflex arc? Trace the sequence of events that occur when you accidentally touch a hot object. Draw a neat, labelled diagram of a reflex arc.

Q.35 [3 Marks] (Ozone Depletion & Food Chains):
     (a) What is biological magnification? Explain with an example of an aquatic food chain.
     (b) How is the ozone layer formed in the stratosphere? Write the equation and explain why its depletion is a cause for global concern.

Q.36 [5 Marks] (Human Heart & Circulation OR Excretory System):
     (a) Draw a neat diagram of the Human Heart and label: Left atrium, Left ventricle, Right atrium, Right ventricle, Aorta, and Vena Cava.
     (b) Explain the concept of double circulation in human beings. Why is it necessary to separate oxygenated and deoxygenated blood in mammals and birds?

     ----------------------------------------------------------------------
     [OR CHOICE]
     (a) Draw a neat, labelled diagram of a Nephron (functional unit of kidney).
     (b) Describe the three main steps involved in the process of urine formation: (i) Glomerular filtration, (ii) Tubular reabsorption, (iii) Tubular secretion.
     (c) What is the function of artificial kidney (hemodialysis)?
