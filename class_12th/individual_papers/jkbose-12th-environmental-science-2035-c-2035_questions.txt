SECTION A : OBJECTIVE TYPE QUESTIONS
[Q. 01 to Q. 04 [4 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.01 [1 Mark] (Atmosphere & Climate Change):
     Which international treaty was signed in 1987 to control the emission of ozone-depleting chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)?

     (A) Kyoto Protocol
     (B) Montreal Protocol
     (C) Paris Agreement
     (D) Ramsar Convention

Q.02 [1 Mark] (Water Quality & Eutrophication):
     The accelerated aging of a water body resulting from nutrient enrichment (phosphates and nitrates) is known as:

     (A) Biomagnification
     (B) Cultural Eutrophication
     (C) Bioaccumulation
     (D) Thermal Stratification

Q.03 [1 Mark] (Biodiversity Conservation):
     Which of the following is an ex-situ method of biodiversity conservation?

     (A) National Park
     (B) Wildlife Sanctuary
     (C) Botanical Garden / Seed Bank
     (D) Biosphere Reserve

Q.04 [1 Mark] (Environmental Legislation):
     In which year was the Environment (Protection) Act enacted in India?

================================================================================

Q.05 [2 Marks] (Ozone Layer Depletion):

Q.06 [2 Marks] (Solid Waste & Hazardous Waste):

Q.07 [2 Marks] (Environmental Impact Assessment):
