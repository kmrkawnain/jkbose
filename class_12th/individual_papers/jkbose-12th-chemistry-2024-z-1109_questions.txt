Q.01 [1 Mark] (Solutions - Colligative Properties):
     Which of the following aqueous solutions will exhibit the highest boiling point elevation?

     (A) 0.1 M Glucose
     (B) 0.1 M NaCl
     (C) 0.1 M BaCl₂
     (D) 0.1 M Urea

Q.02 [1 Mark] (Electrochemistry):
     The unit of molar conductivity (Λₘ) is:

     (A) S cm² mol⁻¹
     (B) S cm⁻¹ mol⁻¹
     (C) S⁻¹ cm² mol
     (D) Ω cm mol⁻¹

Q.03 [1 Mark] (Chemical Kinetics):
     For a first order reaction, the time required for 99.9% completion is how many times the half-life period (t₁/₂)?

     (A) 2 times
     (B) 5 times
     (C) 10 times
     (D) 100 times

Q.04 [1 Mark] (Coordination Compounds):
     The IUPAC name of the complex compound [Co(NH₃)₅Cl]Cl₂ is:

     (A) Pentaamminechlorocobalt(III) chloride
     (B) Pentaamminechlorocobalt(II) chloride
     (C) Chloropentaamminecobalt(III) dichloride
     (D) Cobalt(III) pentaamminechloride

Q.05 [1 Mark] (d- and f-Block Elements):
     What is Lanthanoid Contraction and what is its main chemical consequence on 4d and 5d transition elements?

Q.06 [1 Mark] (Biomolecules):
     What is the primary difference between DNA and RNA with respect to their sugar moiety and pyrimidine bases?

Q.10 [3 Marks] (Solutions - Nernst Equation):
     Calculate the EMF of the following Galvanic cell at 298 K:
     Mg(s) | Mg²⁺(0.001 M) || Cu²⁺(0.0001 M) | Cu(s)
     Given: E°(Mg²⁺/Mg) = -2.37 V, E°(Cu²⁺/Cu) = +0.34 V, [R = 8.314 J/K·mol, F = 96500 C/mol].

Q.11 [3 Marks] (Coordination Chemistry):
     On the basis of Crystal Field Theory (CFT), explain why [CoF₆]³⁻ is a paramagnetic high-spin complex whereas [Co(CN)₆]³⁻ is a diamagnetic low-spin complex.
