Q.01 [1 Mark] (Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants):
     The ploidy level of the Primary Endosperm Nucleus (PEN) in angiosperms after double fertilization is:

     (A) Haploid (n)
     (B) Diploid (2n)
     (C) Triploid (3n)

     Which enzyme synthesizes the RNA primer required during DNA replication?

     (A) DNA Polymerase I
     (B) DNA Primase
     (C) Topoisomerase
     (D) DNA Ligase

Q.04 [2 Marks] (Ecosystem):

Q.05 [3 Marks] (Molecular Genetics - Lac Operon):

Q.06 [5 Marks] (Plant Reproduction):
