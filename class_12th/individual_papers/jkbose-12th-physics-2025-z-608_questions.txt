[Q. 01 to Q. 06 [6 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.01 [1 Mark] (Electrostatics - Gauss Law):
     An electric dipole of dipole moment p is placed in a uniform electric field E at an angle of 90°. The torque acting on the dipole is:

     (A) pE
     (B) 0
     (C) -pE
     (D) pE/2

Q.02 [1 Mark] (Current Electricity - Drift Velocity):
     When a potential difference V is applied across a conductor of length L, the drift velocity of free electrons is proportional to:

     (A) V and L
     (B) V and 1/L
     (C) 1/V and L
     (D) √V and L

Q.03 [1 Mark] (Electromagnetic Induction):
     Lenz’s law of electromagnetic induction is a consequence of the law of conservation of:

     (A) Charge
     (B) Momentum
     (C) Energy
     (D) Mass

Q.04 [1 Mark] (Wave Optics):
     In Young’s double slit experiment, if the separation between the slits is halved and the distance between the slits and screen is doubled, the fringe width will:

     (A) Remain unchanged
     (B) Be doubled
     (C) Become four-fold
     (D) Be halved

Q.05 [1 Mark] (Dual Nature of Radiation):
     State how the de Broglie wavelength (λ) of an electron varies with its accelerating potential (V).

Q.06 [1 Mark] (Semiconductors):
     Why is a semiconductor damaged by a strong current, and what is the width of the depletion layer in a forward-biased p-n junction?

SECTION B : SHORT ANSWER TYPE QUESTIONS - I
[Q. 07 to Q. 09 [6 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.07 [2 Marks] (Current Electricity - Kirchhoff Rules):
     State Kirchhoff's First Rule (Junction Rule) and Second Rule (Loop Rule). Name the conservation principles on which they are based.

Q.08 [2 Marks] (Magnetism - Moving Charges):
     Derive an expression for the force per unit length between two infinitely long straight parallel conductors carrying currents I₁ and I₂ separated by a distance 'd'. Define 1 Ampere.

Q.09 [2 Marks] (Nuclear Physics):
     Define mass defect and binding energy per nucleon. Draw a graph showing the variation of binding energy per nucleon with mass number (A).

SECTION C : SHORT ANSWER TYPE QUESTIONS - II
[Q. 10 to Q. 13 [12 Marks]]
================================================================================

Q.10 [3 Marks] (Ray Optics - Lens Maker Formula):
     Derive the Lens Maker's Formula 1/f = (μ - 1)(1/R₁ - 1/R₂) for a thin double convex lens of radii of curvature R₁ and R₂.

Q.11 [3 Marks] (Alternating Current - LCR Series Circuit):
     An alternating voltage V = V₀ sin(ωt) is applied across a series LCR circuit. Obtain the impedance (Z) using the phasor diagram and find the condition for electrical resonance.

Q.12 [3 Marks] (Photoelectric Effect):
     State Einstein's photoelectric equation. Explain the characteristics of photoelectric emission (threshold frequency, saturation current, and stopping potential).

Q.13 [3 Marks] (Semiconductor Diodes):
     Explain the working of a full-wave rectifier using two junction diodes with a neat circuit diagram. Draw input and output waveforms.

Q.14 [5 Marks] (Electrostatics - Capacitance & Gauss Law):
     State Gauss's Law in electrostatics.
     Using Gauss's Law, derive an expression for the electric field intensity due to an infinitely long straight uniformly charged wire.
     A parallel plate capacitor with air between the plates has a capacitance of 8 pF. What will be the capacitance if the distance between the plates is reduced by half and the space between them is filled with a dielectric substance of dielectric constant K = 6?

     Explain the principle, construction, and working of an AC Generator with the help of a labelled diagram.
